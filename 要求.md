2.项目内容：

 **AI-Generated Image Detection**

**AI-Generated Image Detection** is the task of distinguishing images synthesized by modern diffusion / GAN models from authentic photographs. Robust detectors are essential for forensics, content moderation, and trust in visual media. 

A laptop-friendly setup: train a ResNet-18 or small ViT on a GenImage subset (or implement the patch-based PSM detector) and study cross-generator generalisation.

**Reference:**

[Ojha et al. — Towards Universal Fake Image Detectors that Generalize Across Generative Models (CVPR 2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10174) (paper)

[GenImage: A Million-Scale Benchmark for Detecting AI-Generated Image](https://genimage-dataset.github.io/) (benchmark / dataset)
