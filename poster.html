📌 Background & Motivation
  • GANs, diffusion models, and autoregressive architectures produce increasingly photorealistic images, raising misinformation risks.
  • Detectors trained on one generator typically fail on unseen generators because each model leaves distinct low-level artifacts.
  • Core challenge: cross-generator generalisation across fundamentally different generation families (GAN vs. diffusion).
  • Goal: Systematically evaluate detection strategies and propose a Mixture-of-Experts framework.

🎯 Key Takeaways
  • Full FT dominates in-distribution (98–99%) but collapses on unseen diffusion generators (52–56%).
  • CLIP KNN generalises best on hard generators (ADM 64.9%, VQDM 64.7%), supporting frozen vision-language features.
  • Patch analysis reveals errors are systematic, not localised — pointing to global feature mismatch.
  • Unsupervised clustering of fine-tuned features achieves 2× purity improvement, validating MoE routing feasibility.
  • Future: MoE framework with GAN expert, diffusion expert, and learned router on detection features.
